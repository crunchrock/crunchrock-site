The house becomes the game.

You paid a stranger in a bathroom thirty dollars for mushrooms. Now an alien named Randy is in your apartment, and every round he rebuilds it.

Three games in the free demo, then a short Rumble With Randy.

Monkey
Bowls

Trippy bowling with a combo chain. Hit crazy combo throws with tricks like bunny hops, 360s and griefing multipliers.

There are zero actual monkeys in this minigame, stop asking about them!

Hot
Boys

The house fills with lava and turns you all into tiny Hot Boys. Get money while shooting your opponents into the lava and staying out of it yourself.

The
Bathrooms

Get lost in an aesthetically cohesive but ultimately hollow and terrifying minigame. Make money on the run from Crazy Randy in a generated nightmare hell maze from hell with extra hell.

Every minigame and round is what you make it. Play it straight, strategic or only grief your opponent. Randy gets his juices whether anybody wins or loses.

My clients require a litre of loosh. You play my games, you fill my jar. Then we finish each other off.

Randy has quotas.

He is in the apartment holding a big jar and talking about your juices. He is not a monster about it. Every round pours loosh into the jar, and the fuller it gets, the stranger your house looks.

When the jar is full, it all ends in a Rumble With Randy. The demo has the short version. There will be eggs.
