A first-person party game in one apartment, hosted by the alien who lives there. Free demo for two, on one couch or over Steam.

The free demo is in with Valve for review right now. The Steam page is live and takes wishlists while we wait. A new trailer is being cut from the demo build.

The apartment does not stay an apartment.

Two of you wake up in loaner bodies in a flat that belongs to Zlorp. He wants a litre of loosh, which is the stuff you leak when you are winning, losing, or getting hit with a bucket. He runs games to get it. The rooms rearrange themselves for whichever game he has picked, and between rounds you can pick up nearly anything and throw it at nearly anyone.

The walls change with the night. The radio has stations. The jar can be inspected up close. None of it is load-bearing and all of it is throwable.

Pick a game. The room rebuilds itself.

You walk up to the picker. Zlorp announces the game. The furniture leaves, the lanes arrive, and the mushrooms come to watch.

Three games in the demo. Rounds run short enough that the rematch starts before anyone has finished complaining about the last one.

Monkey
Bowls

The living room becomes two lanes. The pins are bananas. The ball is whatever you were holding. Zlorp calls the game from the ceiling and has opinions about your form.

Strike or gutter, he bottles it either way.

Floor
Is Lava

You are shrunk to flea size and the floor goes molten. The furniture is the map now. Climb the fridge, ride the flying food, and take the other flea's island while they are not looking.

Every dry second pays. Height pays more.

Zlorp
Dash

Orders come in. You raid the fridge for the right food, pack your box, and ship it through the microwave. Your rival is packing from the same fridge and will happily knock your carrot into the sink.

A fuller box tips better. A wrong box tips nothing.

Early Access takes it to seven games in a bigger house, for four players. Mecha Chameleon and Snack Heist are next in, then Gun Box Gallery, plus one we haven't settled on yet.

No points race. Every win pours loosh into one shared litre. When it brims, Zlorp comes all the way through, and the apartment stops pretending to be somewhere you have been before.

The colours you see at the end are the ones the room was hiding the whole time.
