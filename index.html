the game

Wacky action. Tight loops.

Bad Shrooms is a psychedelic first-person party game in one rearrangeable apartment. You and a friend ate mushrooms that were not fine. Zlorp moved in and turned game night into a hustle.

Grab, throw, steal the pad, ship the box, fill the jar. Rounds are short. Betrayal is the mechanic. Local vertical split or Steam online - both seats play for real.
